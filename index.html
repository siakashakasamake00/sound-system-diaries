03 — About

I'M
SIAKA.

I want to show you who's behind Sound System Diaries.

Sound System Diaries was born from a simple passion: for Roots, Dub and Sound System culture.

I go to the dances, I listen, I meet people, I film, I shoot photos and try to capture everything that's often hard to put into words: the vibrations, the energy of the sound, the people, the connections and those moments that make every session different from the others.

I don't want to tell this culture from the outside. I want to live it, know it, document it and share it.

Every video, every photo and every moment I collect is a small fragment of a journey I'm living personally, sound after sound.

Sound System Diaries is my way of giving value to these moments, discovering new sounds, meeting new people and continuing to learn from a culture that inspires me deeply.

This is what's behind Sound System Diaries.

Not just images. Not just music. But experiences, people, vibrations and moments worth remembering.
